## 1. Regras globais / Global rules

Use estas regras em todos os prompts, mesmo quando não repetidas integralmente.

### Global look block
```text
Authentic 16mm motion-picture realism, organic film grain, subtle halation, realistic exposure rolloff, tactile domestic textures, lived-in kitchen, restrained procedural noir atmosphere, natural animal anatomy, observational framing, no glossy digital sharpness, no cartoon, no CGI look.
```

### Global negative block
```text
No people visible, no anthropomorphic acting, no pet-commercial look, no detective costume, no props associated with human detectives, no comedy, no fantasy, no exaggerated cinematic lighting, no artificial vintage filter, no digital graphic overlays.
```

### Character lock
- **Tsuki:** female Miniature Schnauzer, salt-and-pepper wiry coat, silver-gray brows, cream beard, caramel mouth stain, dark brown eyes, natural curved tail.
- **Simba:** senior Siamese seal point male, cream body, dark seal mask, large triangular ears, ice-blue almond eyes, calm witness energy.

### Spatial lock
- SH002 entry line = `SOUTH`
- SH003/SH004 evidence line = `WEST`
- SH005/SH006/SH007 interrogation line = `NORTH`
- SH008 closeout line = `NORTH / WEST evidence axis`
- SH001/SH009 = dossier on table, non-kitchen insert world

### Dimensional lock
Use `assets/references/dimensional/Tsuki e Simba - referencia visual e dimencional.png` as a scale guide whenever body scale is visible.

```text
Tsuki must read as a small compact Miniature Schnauzer, never medium-sized. Simba must read as a slim Siamese with lighter mass and slightly smaller total presence than Tsuki. Keep both animals modest in relation to cabinet height, chair legs, bowl size, window sill, and door proportions.
```

## SH001 — Dossiê abre

### Still / Start frame
```text
Use the dossier closed reference. Top-down macro insert, tight on the typed label of a heavy closed kraft dossier folder resting on a dark aged wooden table. Weathered cardboard fibers, oxidized metal grommets, warm tungsten side light from the left, restrained shadows, serious archival mood. The label reads CASO Nº 001 — O Biscoito Que Não Estava Lá. No stamp yet. Authentic 16mm realism.
```

### Still / End frame
```text
Use the closed dossier start frame as reference. Same top-down angle, camera slightly pulled back. The folder is now fully open, revealing yellowed case papers, typewritten sheets, and pencil notes inside. Same wood table, same tungsten side light, same analog 16mm character. No human hands.
```

### Motion prompt
```text
The closed dossier cover opens smoothly on its own while the camera performs a slow motivated zoom out from macro label detail to a wider top-down view of the fully open folder. Motion must feel physical and restrained, not magical or theatrical. Preserve paper weight, hinge resistance, tungsten light direction, and tactile 16mm realism.
```

### Still / End frame
```text
Use Tsuki master and kitchen south reference. Low-angle wide shot at 25cm above floor level, looking into the kitchen from the southern doorway. Tsuki enters from the left in a measured investigative walk and then holds in frame, facing toward the west evidence line. Pine floorboards, soft north daylight spill, modest lived-in kitchen, restrained domestic color palette, authentic 16mm realism.

### Motion prompt
```text
Tsuki enters the frame from the southern doorway with steady deliberate steps. Camera tracks subtly and observationally at low dog-eye height, never flashy. She slows and settles into alert stillness, focused on something deeper inside the kitchen. Natural canine gait only.
```

## SH003 — Tsuki encontra a tigela vazia

### Still / Start frame
```text
Use Tsuki master, kitchen west reference, and bowl reference. Medium close-up at 20cm from the floor on the west work-counter line. Tsuki is approaching the empty cream ceramic bowl on a woven jute mat. The bowl is conspicuously empty. Late-afternoon side light from the north window skims the bowl rim and Tsuki’s cream beard. Authentic 16mm documentary realism.
```

### Still / End frame
```text
Use the start frame as reference. Same west line, same 20cm camera height. Tsuki has stopped with her snout lowered directly over the empty bowl rim, examining it with intense forensic seriousness. Preserve exact coat, beard, brows, eyes, and bowl identity. No crumbs emphasized in this frame; the absence is the point.
```

### Motion prompt
```text
Tsuki completes her approach, slows, lowers her snout toward the empty bowl, and performs a minimal investigative sniff. Camera remains almost static, with only subtle handheld breathing if needed. The tension comes from precision and stillness.
```

```text
Use kitchen west macro reference and bowl macro reference. The floor is meticulously clean: zero crumbs, zero saliva, zero wet marks, zero disturbance. Raking natural daylight reveals wood grain. Authentic 16mm macro realism.

### Still / End frame
```text

```

### Motion prompt
```text
Hold on the macro evidence frame with almost no motion, only a subtle focus drift or microscopic breathing shift across the floor texture. This shot is forensic confirmation of absence, not discovery of a clue.

## SH005 — Interrogatório silencioso

### Still / Start frame
```text
Use Tsuki master, Simba master, and kitchen north reference. Low-angle medium two-shot looking toward the north window. Tsuki stands below on the floor, looking up at a 45-degree angle with unwavering focus. Simba sits above on the windowsill or counter line, looking down calmly. Strong vertical asymmetry, restrained afternoon window light, domestic realism, authentic 16mm tone.
```

### Still / End frame
```text
Use the start frame as reference. Same composition, but camera is 15 to 20 percent closer, compressing the distance between Tsuki below and Simba above. Neither animal changes pose significantly. The tension increases through framing, not acting.
```

### Motion prompt
```text
Sustain a silent confrontation between Tsuki and Simba. Both remain almost perfectly still while the camera performs a slow motivated push-in. Preserve the asymmetry: young dog investigator below, older cat witness above. No barking, no overt reaction, no humanized body language.
```
