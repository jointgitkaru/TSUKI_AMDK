## Core Visual Identity

The visual language of the project must convey:

```
CINEMATIC
INTIMATE
OBSERVATIONAL
NATURALISTIC
PHYSICAL
TACTILE
LIVED-IN
IMPERFECT
FILMIC
```

---

## 16mm Film Character

The base aesthetic must preserve:

```
Analog 16mm film character.
Organic film grain.
Subtle texture.
Natural exposure variation.
Soft optical imperfections.
Subtle halation around practical highlights.
Tactile material surfaces.
Natural shadow detail.
Physical lighting sources.
Avoid overly clean digital sharpness.
Avoid glossy commercial imagery.
```

### Important Rule

The 16mm aesthetic must NOT mean adding:
- Excessive noise
- Artificial filters
- Exaggerated degradation
- Random stains
- "Vintage effect" appearance

The goal is **organic cinematic character**, not a synthetic filter applied on top of the image.

## Camera Bible

### Approved Camera States

```
LOCKED_OFF
STATIC_OBSERVATIONAL
SUBTLE_HANDHELD
SLOW_PUSH_IN
SLOW_LATERAL_MOVEMENT
FOLLOW
OBSERVATIONAL_TRACK
```

### Camera Movement Rule

Every movement must respond to a clear narrative motivation:

```
CHARACTER  → movement motivated by character action
EMOTION    → movement motivated by emotional progression
SPACE      → movement motivated by spatial discovery
NARRATIVE  → movement motivated by narrative emphasis
```

If no clear reason exists: **KEEP THE CAMERA STILL.**

### Prohibited Camera Behaviors

```
RANDOM ORBIT
UNMOTIVATED CRANE
CONSTANT PUSH-IN
EXTREME CINEMATIC MOVEMENT
EXCESSIVE DOLLY MOVEMENT
ARTIFICIAL CAMERA INSTABILITY
UNNECESSARY SLOW MOTION
```

## Shot Vocabulary

```
ECU  — Extreme Close Up (Macro evidence, muzzle, eye details)
CU   — Close Up (Head portraits, focused gaze)
MCU  — Medium Close Up (Character down to chest/front paws)
MS   — Medium Shot (Full animal or two-shot confrontation)
MLS  — Medium Long Shot (Scene with environmental context / background human)
WS   — Wide Shot (Full room / spatial relationships)
EWS  — Extreme Wide Shot (Master establishing)
OTS  — Over The Shoulder (Animal perspective POV)
POV  — Point of View (Direct animal eye-line)
INSERT — Detail cutaway (Empty bowl, crumbs, grease mark, dossier)
DETAIL — Macro close-up on physical evidence
```

Every shot must declare its type.

## Lighting Bible

Light must have a comprehensible, motivated physical origin.

### Preferred
```
PRACTICAL LIGHT (Tungsten lamps, ceiling fixtures)
WINDOW LIGHT (Soft daytime / direct late-afternoon daylight)
NATURAL LIGHT (Ambient fill)
ROOM LIGHT (Domestic interior balance)
MOTIVATED LIGHT (Directional source consistent with architecture)
```

### Prohibited / Avoid Adding Automatically
```
DRAMATIC BACKLIGHT (without physical light fixture)
VOLUMETRIC RAYS (exaggerated god rays)
UNEXPLAINED COLOR LIGHT (neon, synthetic tints)
COMMERCIAL BEAUTY LIGHTING
```

## Location Bible (A Cozinha — Canonical Coordinates)

Classification: `[LOCATION_REFERENCE]` — **Level 01, CORE CANON**

### Kitchen Geography

The kitchen operates on four cardinal reference walls, locked in `location_kitchen.md` and `TSUKI_AMDK_KITCHEN_[Norte/Sul/Leste/Oeste].png`:

```
NORTH — Light Wall (Janela & Parapeito)
  Large dark iron-frame window admitting strong warm afternoon daylight
  Simba's elevated observation perch on windowsill

SOUTH — Transit / Exit Wall (Porta & Corredor)
  Dark wood door leading to the hallway/corridor
  Primary investigative POV entry point for Tsuki

WEST — Work Wall (Bancada de Trabalho)
  Full rustic work counter: sink, stove, shelved ceramic jars, lower wooden cabinets
  Primary investigative "working line" of the kitchen (crumbs, floor base)

EAST — Social / Dining Table Wall (Mesa & Estação de Alimentação)
  Sturdy wooden dining table, chairs, framed family portraits on aged plaster
  Pet feeding station area (ceramic bowl and water dish on floor)
```

### Spatial Anchor Zero-Point

A small cream ceramic bowl on the wooden floor appears in a consistent position.
This bowl is the room's spatial zero-point — all blocking, character positioning,
and camera placement are calculated relative to it.

### Assigning Cardinal Direction to a Shot

Every kitchen shot must declare which cardinal direction it uses as `LOCATION_REFERENCE`:

```
North → window-dependent beats, Simba on windowsill, direct afternoon daylight
South → transit/exit beats, establishing shots looking toward/from the corridor
West  → work-counter beats, investigating underneath counter, crumbs at cabinet base
East  → dining-table beats, background human passage, pet feeding area
```

## Sound Bible

Sound is part of the universe identity. Every scene must consider:

```
ROOM TONE
ENVIRONMENT
DISTANT SOUNDS (Clock ticking, refrigerator hum, distant traffic)
OBJECT INTERACTION (Bowl shifting, cardboard opening, floor creaking)
CHARACTER MOVEMENT (Soft paw pads on pine floorboards, claws clicking)
BREATHING (Sniffing, calm purr/breathing)
SILENCE (Used as a dramatic procedural tool)
```

### Music Rule

Do not add music automatically. Ask: *What does the music add that the environment does not?*
The series score uses **minor key piano** — restrained, sparse, and melancholic.

---

## Performance & Animal Realism

Characters must never demonstrate human acting or anthropomorphic behavior:

- Move with realistic canine/feline anatomy and believable physical weight.
- Natural reaction times and unforced postures.
- Tsuki: serious, concentrated, procedural, no cute expressions.
- Simba: still, observant, slow blinks, dignified witness.
